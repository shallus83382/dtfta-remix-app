# Privacy Policy (DTFTA)

Last updated: 2026-05-05

## 1. Who We Are

DTFTA is a Shopify app that helps merchants manage custom product workflows, fulfillment operations, and pre-fulfillment billing controls.

## 2. Data We Process

To provide app functionality, we may process:

- Shop account and configuration data (shop domain, app setup state)
- Product and variant data required for customization/fulfillment workflows
- Order and fulfillment data required to process merchant operations
- Limited customer/order metadata contained in Shopify order payloads
- Billing metadata necessary for Shopify managed app billing and usage charging

We do not collect or store customer payment card numbers.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data strictly to:

- Provide product customization and fulfillment features
- Sync and process Shopify webhook events
- Enforce billing rules before fulfillment when enabled
- Troubleshoot, audit, and secure app operations

## 4. Data Retention

We retain data only as necessary to provide and support the app, meet legal obligations, and maintain operational records.

When uninstall/redaction requests are received, we process deletion or anonymization in accordance with Shopify requirements and applicable law.

## 5. Shopify GDPR Webhooks

This app supports Shopify privacy webhooks:

- `customers/data_request`
- `customers/redact`
- `shop/redact`

Upon receipt, we process applicable export/redaction/deletion actions in our backend systems.

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard controls such as authentication, request signing/verification, and access restriction to protect merchant data.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell merchant or customer personal data.  
We may use infrastructure/service providers necessary to operate the app (hosting, storage, logging, etc.) under appropriate safeguards.

## 8. Your Rights

Merchants can contact us to request information related to data handling and privacy rights, subject to legal limitations.
